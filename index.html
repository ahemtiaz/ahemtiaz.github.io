Verifiable code generation in Rust with Verus

Jun 2026 — present

Research Intern, UIUC++ SRSE (remote) · Mentors: Prof. Darko Marinov (UIUC), Prof. Wenxi Wang (University of Virginia)

  • Extending VeriContest, a benchmark of 946 LeetCode and Codeforces problems that pairs each natural-language problem statement with expert-validated formal specifications, judge-accepted Rust code, and Verus-checked proofs.
  • Built a specification-testing pipeline that runs candidate formal specifications against positive and mutated-negative test suites. This surfaces the failure mode that verification alone cannot catch: a specification that is under- or over-constrained relative to the problem, yet still verifies against its own implementation.
  • Developing automated test-case generation for competitive-programming problems to strengthen the benchmark's negative-example coverage.

Natural-language auditing of financial documents

Apr 2026 — present

Research Assistant, BUET CSE Incubator · Supervisor: Prof. Anindya Iqbal · Published as [C1]

  • Bank statements are the primary evidentiary record in financial auditing, but they reach auditors as heterogeneous PDFs — some digitally typeset, some scanned — that today get transcribed into spreadsheets by hand.
  • Built a multi-tier parser that routes pages between a deterministic grid extractor (PyMuPDF) and an OCR/layout engine (LlamaParse), then repairs extraction errors by reconciling the running-balance identity — catching sign inversions, decimal shifts, and debit/credit column swaps before schema standardization. Evaluated on 23 statements from 20 commercial banks: 75 pages, 1,672 transactions.
  • Benchmarked five context-injection strategies for the NL-to-SQL audit layer — zero-context, retrieval-augmented, and execution-feedback with an agentic validation loop — across model generations on 150 hand-authored audit queries, quantifying how much of the usual architectural scaffolding newer models render unnecessary.

LLM-based building-code compliance checking

Apr 2026 — present

Research Assistant, BUET CSE Incubator · Supervisor: Prof. Anindya Iqbal · In progress

  • An agent that compiles Bangladesh National Building Code clauses into executable Python checkers that run against IFC building models.
  • The central problem is that there are no labels: asking an LLM whether its own generated checker is correct is circular and self-preferring. The agent instead manufactures ground truth — it perturbs IFC models into fixtures with known verdicts, and accepts a checker only when it kills every injected violation, stays silent on compliant variants, and correctly reports unknown or not_applicable on degraded ones.
  • Targeting a submission in the LLM-for-code track.

Interpretable deep learning for CRISPR-Cas9 off-target prediction

Dec 2023 — Mar 2025

Undergraduate Thesis, Department of CSE, BUET · Supervisor: Prof. M. Sohel Rahman · Submitted as [J1]

  • Designed CRISMER: a multi-branch CNN that extracts k-mer features from encoded DNA–sgRNA pairs, coupled with transformer blocks that capture long-range dependencies across the pair.
  • Achieved a 2–8% improvement in PR-AUC and F1 over recent state-of-the-art baselines on genome-wide off-target datasets, after surveying CNN, RNN/LSTM/GRU, transformer, and BERT-based architectures.
  • Applied DeepSHAP and Integrated Gradients to attribute predictions back to mismatch positions, and used the resulting model to drive guide-RNA optimization.
  • Supported by the RISE BUET Students' Research Grant.
